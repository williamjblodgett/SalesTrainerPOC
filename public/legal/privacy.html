DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW

Privacy notice

Last updated: August 5, 2026. This draft describes the intended privacy practices for Suadence and is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific notice approved by counsel.

Data we process

Account details, organization membership, consent-confirmed transcripts, structured customer intelligence, practice-session turns, evaluations, usage records, and security audit metadata. Raw voice audio is not stored by default.

Why we process it

To operate customer-authorized revenue intelligence, training, coaching, security, support, and compliance workflows. Customer content is not used to train shared models by default.

Control and retention

Customers retain ownership of submitted content and derived assets. Authorized administrators can request export or deletion subject to identity verification, contractual retention, legal holds, and backup-expiry procedures.

Service providers

Approved infrastructure, authentication, AI, scanning, monitoring, and support providers may process limited data under contract. See the subprocessor disclosure.

Contact

A production privacy contact and data-subject request channel must be established before accepting customer data.
