# Data processing agreement checklist

Status: negotiation checklist, not legal advice or an executed agreement.

- Parties, controller/processor roles, documented instructions, and service purpose
- Customer ownership and no shared-model training by default
- Data categories, data subjects, processing operations, and duration
- Confidentiality and least-privilege personnel access
- Technical and organizational security measures
- Approved subprocessors, regions, notice, and objection process
- International-transfer mechanism and supplementary measures
- Incident notification process and contractual timeline
- Data-subject request assistance
- DPIA and regulator-cooperation assistance
- Return, deletion, backup expiry, and legal-hold handling
- Audit rights, security reports, and penetration-test treatment
- Source-provider retention responsibility for Gong, Zoom, Teams, Chorus, Salesforce, and similar systems
- Termination, liability, governing law, and order-of-precedence terms

Counsel must approve the DPA, privacy notice, consent mapping, and jurisdiction-specific recording rules before live customer ingestion.
