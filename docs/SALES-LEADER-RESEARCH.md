# What sales leaders need from Suadence

Research reviewed July 22, 2026. This synthesis emphasizes current reports and durable findings from sales leadership, enablement, and capability-building research.

## Strongest product requirements

1. **Turn practice into a manager action queue.** Managers know coaching matters but lack time and diagnostic insight. Suadence should surface the few reps, moments, and competencies that deserve human attention instead of asking managers to review every call.
2. **Measure observable behavior, not activity.** Completion counts are operating metrics, not proof of readiness. Certification should require repeated evidence across anchored skills; low-confidence and overridden scores should remain visible.
3. **Practice in the flow of work.** Short, role-specific drills tied to a rep's next conversation are more useful than isolated training events. Recommended drills should follow a weak moment immediately and re-test the same skill in a different scenario.
4. **Make managers the reinforcement layer.** AI can scale practice and first-pass feedback, but sales leaders need calibration controls, score overrides, team themes, coaching notes, and an explicit weekly coaching cadence.
5. **Connect readiness to business outcomes.** Leaders ultimately need to compare skill improvement with pipeline quality, stage conversion, ramp time, win rate, and forecast outcomes. Product analytics should keep correlation separate from causal claims.
6. **Support different selling roles and seniority.** SDR, AE, enterprise AE, account manager, sales engineer, and frontline manager conversations require different scenarios, buyer power, complexity, and criteria.
7. **Ground simulations in the company's market reality.** Transcript-derived language, actual objections, persona evidence, products, competitive alternatives, and methodology improve credibility. AI-generated content must remain reviewable and sourced.
8. **Protect trust.** Reps need clear rules about who can see transcripts and scores. Leaders need retention controls, audit history, stable rubric versions, and separation between AI evaluation and manager judgment.

## Recommended next product increments

### Highest value

- Manager coaching inbox with AI confidence, severity, and business priority
- Certification paths by role and segment
- Scenario assignments triggered by weak competencies
- Rubric calibration sessions using shared transcripts
- Transcript evidence aggregation across several customer calls
- CRM outcome overlay for stage progression and win rate

### Important trust and operations work

- Per-user and per-source retention settings
- Rep-visible score appeal and manager response
- Audit log for persona, scenario, rubric, and score changes
- Bias and consistency monitoring by evaluator prompt/model version
- Cost controls and usage forecasts for voice practice

### Avoid

- Public leaderboards
- Opaque single-number scores
- Coaching during a live simulation
- Auto-publishing transcript-derived personas
- Treating call outcome as a proxy for seller skill
- Claiming causation from practice and revenue correlations
